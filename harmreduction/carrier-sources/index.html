Carrier Sources

What's Actually Documented

Transparency note: Most published reagent guides assume powder or blotter — not oils, tinctures, or glycerin. The entries in the testing log marked with carriers other than powder are based on two things: (1) directly documented cases like the Bunk Police propylene glycol study, and (2) general matrix interference science. They are working hypotheses, not confirmed results. This page shows exactly where the data comes from.
